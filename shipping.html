Shipping & Delivery

Every ChapterByEmm order is a physical printed T-shirt, made after you order and shipped from our studio in Pakistan.

Where We Deliver

We currently ship nationwide across Pakistan, including all major cities and most surrounding areas. If you're unsure whether we deliver to your area, message us on WhatsApp before ordering and we'll confirm.

Order Processing

Every tee is printed to order rather than pulled from a shelf. Printing and quality-checking typically takes 2–4 business days. Custom or personalised orders take a little longer — 3–5 business days — because we confirm a proof with you before printing.

Delivery Exceptions

Occasionally, deliveries to remote areas, during public holidays, or affected by weather may take longer than the estimates above. If your order is significantly delayed, message us and we'll follow up with the courier on your behalf.

Cash on Delivery

Cash on Delivery is available nationwide — you pay in cash when your order arrives. Bank Transfer is also accepted; online card and wallet payment is coming soon.
