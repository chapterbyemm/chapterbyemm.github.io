Because custom tees are printed to your exact request and confirmed with a proof beforehand, they can only be returned or exchanged if there is a fault or printing error on our side.

How to Start a Return or Exchange

  1. Message us on WhatsApp or email with your order number and the reason for the return.
  2. We'll confirm eligibility and the next steps, including where to send the item back if needed.
  3. Once received and checked, we'll process your exchange or refund.
