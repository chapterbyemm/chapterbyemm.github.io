Your words, names, dates or your own idea — set by hand in one of our signature typefaces and printed on the same premium cotton blanks as the rest of the shop.

How it works

Four simple steps

  1. Tell us your idea. Fill in the form below with your wording, style, size and colour.
  2. We reply with a proof. Within 1–2 business days you'll get a mock-up and a firm quote.
  3. You approve it. Nothing is printed until you're happy — small tweaks are free.
  4. We print and ship. Personalised tees are printed in 3–5 business days, then dispatched with tracking.

Personalised tees start at $42. Bulk pricing available from 6 shirts — family reunions, baby showers, small businesses and team gifts welcome.
