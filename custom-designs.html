How It Works

Four Simple Steps

  1. Tell us your idea. Fill in the form below with your wording, style, size and colour.
  2. We reply with a proof. Within 1–2 business days you'll get a mock-up and a firm quote on WhatsApp or email.
  3. You approve it. Nothing is printed until you're happy — small tweaks are free.
  4. We print and ship. Custom tees are printed in 3–5 business days, then dispatched nationwide.

Pricing depends on placement, colours and quantity — we'll confirm your exact price with the quote. Bulk pricing available from 6 shirts for family occasions, small businesses and group gifts.
