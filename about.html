Our Story

Every Design Begins a New Chapter

Every person is living through a different chapter of life — and ChapterByEmm's T-shirts are designed to express those moments.

From Emm

Every chapter in life begins with a small step, and this is mine.

I'm a women's health physiotherapist by profession, someone who spends her days helping others heal, grow, and feel stronger in their own bodies. But beyond my work, I've always carried a quiet love for art, painting, creating, and turning simple ideas into something meaningful and beautiful.

For a long time, that creative side of me stayed in the background… until one day, I decided to give it a space of its own. With hope in my heart and countless prayers, I started this journey on Etsy, my little corner where creativity meets purpose.

“ChapterByEmm” isn't just a shop. It's a reflection of new beginnings, of choosing to start even when you're unsure, and of believing that something beautiful can come from simply trying.

Every design you see here is created with care, intention, and love. My goal is simple — to make things that you'll not only use or wear, but genuinely enjoy… pieces that feel a little special, a little personal, and a little like you.

That love for design has now grown into something you can wear: physical, printed T-shirts, made to order and shipped across Pakistan — a new chapter for a brand that began with digital art on Etsy.

I'm still learning, still growing, and still writing this chapter — but I'm grateful you're here, being a part of it.

Here's to new beginnings, creativity, and finding joy in the little things.
— Emm, founder
