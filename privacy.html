Information We Collect

When you place an order, contact us, or sign up for our newsletter, we may collect your name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, and delivery address.

How We Use Your Information

We use this information to process and deliver your order, respond to your enquiries, and — only if you opt in — send occasional emails about new prints and restocks. We do not sell your information to third parties.

Cash on Delivery & Payment Information

We do not store card details. Cash on Delivery and Bank Transfer orders are confirmed directly with you; any future online payment gateway will process payment details securely through its own provider, not through ChapterByEmm's servers.

Cookies & Local Storage

This website stores your bag and wishlist in your browser's local storage so they're there when you come back. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Your Rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, at any time by contacting us.
