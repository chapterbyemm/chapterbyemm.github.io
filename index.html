Our story

Every person is living through a different chapter

ChapterByEmm began with a simple thought: we are all somewhere in the middle of a story. A new job, a new baby, a hard year that finally softened, a decision that changed everything.

I started printing T-shirts because I wanted something wearable for those moments — not loud slogans, but quiet lines that feel like a friend noticing where you are. Each design is drawn, set and printed in small batches on cotton blanks I've worn and washed myself before ever putting them in the shop.

— Emm, founder
