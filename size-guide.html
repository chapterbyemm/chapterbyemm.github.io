Sizing

Size Guide

All measurements are in inches and taken from the finished garment. If you're between two sizes, size up for a looser, more draped look, or size down for a closer fit.

Each product page also lists its specific fit — relaxed, oversized, or our more fitted cut — alongside this chart.

How to Measure

Three Quick Measurements

Use a soft measuring tape, or lay a T-shirt you already own flat and measure that instead.

1. Chest

Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape level and snug but not tight.

2. Body Length

Measure from the highest point of the shoulder straight down to where you'd like the hem to sit.

3. Sleeve

Measure from the shoulder seam to the end of the sleeve.
